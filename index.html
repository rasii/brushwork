Video effects

    
    
    
    
    
    
Applies to every video. Match sets all five for you: open the finished painting
      (or a progress photo) in the picture window, tap Match, then drag a box around the painting in the video.
      Zoom the picture window in to match just part of it.

    

       

  









  

    
Time split

    
    

    
Painting is time with the brush at the canvas where the painting visibly changed
      (set by the slider above). Palette is hand movement over the palette camera. Talking overlaps the others.

    

  







  

    

    

    
Processing has the PC analyse it and make a prepped copy: about 20 minutes for a 3-hour
      session. It plays straight away, and the skipping modes switch on as the analysis catches up.
      Just watch plays it as it is, with no skipping and nothing started.

    

       

  







  

    
Speed

    

      Playback speed
      

        
        
        
      

      For everything you watch, in every mode, including skipped parts you tap into. Fast-forwarded parts keep their own speed (Settings). The voice keeps its pitch.
    

    

      Touch and hold the video to play at
      

        
        
      

      Let go to go back to the playback speed. Works while paused too. A slow speed is handy for studying a stroke. Sound is off above 4×.
